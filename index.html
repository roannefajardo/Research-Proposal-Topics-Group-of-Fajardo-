Topic 01 · Problem Statement

Inefficient Library Seat Management and Study Space Utilization

Campus libraries offer only a limited number of seats, yet demand peaks sharply during exam periods and deadline weeks. Without a way to reserve or track seat use, spots are claimed indefinitely by students who are away or asleep, while others are turned away at the door. The result is an overcrowded, unpredictable space that fails the students who need it most.

The Challenge

Problems

Students sleep instead of studying.

Students occupy seats for hours.

The library becomes overcrowded during peak times.

Limited seats for students needing study space.

Manual monitoring burdens librarians.

Topic 01 · Proposed System

CCA Library Seat Reservation and Management System

The CCA Library Seat Reservation and Management System gives students a way to reserve a seat in advance and gives library staff visibility into how seats are actually being used. By replacing manual headcounts with a structured reservation and monitoring process, the system keeps the library organized even during its busiest hours.

The Proposal

Solution

How it helps

The system organizes seat reservations before arrival, limits occupancy time, and manages seat availability. It helps maintain a quiet and productive study environment while making library management more efficient for both students and librarians.

Topic 02 · Problem Statement

Challenges in Tricycle Fare Transparency

Tricycle fares vary from driver to driver, and the posted fare matrices passengers rely on are often outdated by the time fuel prices change. Passengers have no quick way to check what they should be paying, and complaints about overcharging are hard to track down after the fact. This gap in transparency erodes trust between drivers and the passengers they serve.

The Challenge

Problems

Drivers charge different fares.

Posted fare matrix may be outdated.

Fuel price increases cause fare disputes.

Passengers cannot verify correct fares.

No centralized fare information.

Complaints are difficult to monitor.

Topic 02 · Proposed System

Tricycle Live Fare System

The Tricycle Live Fare System puts a single, current fare reference in the hands of both drivers and passengers. Instead of relying on printed matrices that go stale, riders and operators can check the same up-to-date figures, closing the gap that fare disputes come from.

The Proposal

Solution

How it helps

The system provides an updated and official digital fare reference for both passengers and drivers. It promotes transparency, reduces fare disputes, and ensures fair and consistent fare computation.

Topic 03 · Problem Statement

Communication Barriers for Individuals with Hearing Disabilities

For individuals with hearing disabilities, spoken conversations move faster than they can be followed, and identifying who is speaking in a group setting adds another layer of difficulty. Important details are easily missed in classes, meetings, and daily interactions, and there is usually no way to go back and review what was said. These barriers limit full participation in everyday communication.

The Challenge

Problems

Difficulty understanding spoken conversations.

Speakers are difficult to identify.

Fast discussions are hard to follow.

Important information may be missed.

Communication barriers still exist.

Conversations cannot be reviewed easily.

Topic 03 · Proposed System

Disability Aid AI System

The Disability Aid AI System listens to spoken conversation and turns it into live, speaker-identified captions as it happens. By keeping a record that can be reviewed afterward, it gives users a reliable way to follow along in real time and catch anything they may have missed.

The Proposal

Solution

How it helps

The system converts speech into live captions, identifies speakers, and saves conversations for later review. It improves accessibility, enhances communication, and helps users participate confidently in classes, meetings, and daily interactions.
